# === BASIC INCIDENT QUESTIONS ===
What is the IP address of the potentially infected internal host in the LAN? High-severity alerts are your most reliable signal.
What is the hostname of the potentially infected machine in the LAN?
What is the Windows user account name of the potentially infected machine in the LAN?
What is likely the initial infection activity?
How did the infection start?
What are the likely the fake or suspicious domains / URLs for this attack?
What are the suspicious external IP addresses contacted, which might be involved in command-and-control (C2) communication?
